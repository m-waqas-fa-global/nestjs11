### MEAN/MERN stack (Angular/React + Node.js)

The MEAN/MERN stack (Angular/React + Node.js) is exceptionally popular in Baku's modern corporate landscape. However, because Azerbaijan's tech sector is heavily dominated by large enterprise conglomerates and IT software houses, your best approach is to target the specific companies that own, operate, or build software for these exact verticals.

    1. SOCAR (State Oil Company of the Azerbaijan Republic)
    SOCAR is the anchor of the Azeri economy. This giant conglomerate is actively digitizing its entire upstream, downstream, and midstream infrastructure. Their internal IT services division builds and maintains complex ERPs, logistics management systems, and oilfield monitoring software using a mix of Angular, .NET, and Java. They are perennially hiring.

    2. PASHA Holding / PASHA Bank
    This massive holding group owns the country’s largest commercial bank (PASHA Bank) and significant insurance operations. They run one of the most modern, well-funded IT transformations in Azerbaijan, heavily utilizing Angular, Node.js, and cloud-native microservices. They have their own Product Management and Engineering hubs.

    3. AZertel (Bakcell / Nar Mobile subsidiaries)
    Azerbaijan has two major mobile operators, both owned by major Turkish holdings. AZertel is the IT/software arm supporting Nar Mobile. They build and maintain customer-facing mobile apps, CRM integrations, and internal analytics platforms. Telecom is one of the fastest-growing tech verticals in Baku right now.

    4. Innovation Agency Azerbaijan
    Government-backed innovation hubs and accelerators are the fastest-growing source of high-quality software jobs. The Innovation Agency funds startups, hackathons, and mentorship programs. This is where many local graduates get their first exposure to modern cloud-native stacks and cross-border collaboration.

    5. Supermarket & Retail Chains (Romar/Bravo)
    Large retail groups like Romar and the Bravo supermarket chain have invested heavily in building custom inventory management, POS (Point of Sale), and loyalty systems. These companies are often overlooked but have active hiring needs for developers who can integrate supply chain data with cloud-based analytics.
